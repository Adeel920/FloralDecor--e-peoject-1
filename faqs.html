Frequently Asked Questions



What types of flowers do you offer?

At Bouquet Florist, we offer a wide range of fresh flowers including roses, lilies, stocks, snapdragons, foliage and seasonal blooms. We also provide flowers for weddings, events, funerals and special occasions.

How do I place an order for flowers?

You can easily place an order for flowers via our website by selecting your desired bouquet or arrangement, visiting our shop, or contacting us by phone or email.

Do you offer same-day flower delivery?

Yes, we offer same-day flower delivery for orders placed before our 1 PM cutoff time. Please note that this may vary depending on the delivery location, time of day and stock availability.

Do you provide flower arrangements for weddings and events?

Yes, we specialise in providing flower arrangements for weddings, parties, events,and other special occasions. Our team will work closely with you to life and create stunning floral displays that complement your life.

What is your return or exchange policy for flowers?

Our goal is to ensure your complete satisfaction with your floral purchase. If you're not entirely satisfied with your order, please contact us as soon as possible, and we'll do our best to address any concerns or offer a suitable solution.

Can I include a personalised message with my flower delivery?

Absolutely! You can include a personalised message with your flower delivery during the checkout process. Simply enter your message in the provided section, and we'll make sure it's included with your order.
